# Voice profile — seed

This file teaches the model how you write. It is read once, the first time you run
`linkedos draft`, and copied into the `voice_profile` table. Edit it before that first
run, or edit the row afterwards.

Both sections below are required. Keep the `##` headings exactly as they are.

Until you replace the `TODO` markers, `linkedos draft` will warn you that the drafts
will not sound like you.

## Guidelines

Rules about your voice, in your own words. Be specific and be negative where it helps —
"never open with a question" does more work than "be engaging". Some prompts to answer:

- What do you sound like? (dry, warm, blunt, careful, funny)
- How long is a typical post of yours?
- What do you never do? (emoji, hashtags, hooks, humble-brags)
- Which words or phrases are yours? Which are not?
- Who are you writing for, and what do they already know?

## Examples

Three to six of your real past LinkedIn posts, verbatim, best first. These do more to
shape the output than the guidelines above — the model imitates what it sees far more
reliably than what it is told.

Paste each post in full, separated by a blank line and a `---` rule. Do not clean them
up, do not summarise them, and do not write new ones for this file. Posts that performed
